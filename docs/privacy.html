Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection

Mihiraki PDF Utility does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All PDF processing (rendering, splitting, merging, etc.) is performed locally on your device. Your PDF files are never uploaded to any external servers.

2. In-App Purchases

This app includes optional in-app purchases (tipping) to support development. These transactions are handled exclusively by the Google Play Billing system. We do not have access to your payment details (such as credit card numbers).

3. Permissions

The app requests access to your files only for the purpose of opening and saving PDF documents as directed by you. It does not access your files in the background.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
