MihirakiPDFUtility

【Short description】 (Max 80 chars)
Intuitive PDF spread view, splitting, and merging. Perfect for e-books and manga.

【Full description】 (Max 4000 chars)
Mihiraki PDF Utility is a powerful tool designed to help you read and organize PDF files effortlessly on your Android device. It is specifically optimized for double-page spreads, manga, and professional document management.

【Key Features】
■ Spread (Mihiraki) View
View two pages side-by-side. Fully supports Right-to-Left (RTL) reading direction for Japanese books and manga.

■ Advanced Splitting
Split double-page spreads into individual pages. Supports both Vertical (Left/Right) and Horizontal (Top/Bottom) splitting directions.

■ Intuitive Reordering
Organize your PDF by simply dragging thumbnails. Merge multiple files, insert blank pages, and rotate or delete pages with ease.

■ Margin Cropping
Cut off unnecessary margins from scanned documents by specifying a percentage for each side.

■ Security & Metadata
Support for password-protected PDFs. Edit document properties like Title, Author, Subject, and Keywords.

■ Diagnostics & Audit Logs
Run quick in-app diagnostic tests and inspect local audit logs (24-hour retention, privacy-protected with no external transmission, shareable).

■ Desktop Ready (ChromeOS)
Enhanced for large screens and Chromebooks with keyboard shortcuts and mouse context menus.

■ Full-screen Preview
Inspect pages in high resolution with smooth pinch-to-zoom and swipe navigation.

■ Multi-Language Support
Supports 14 languages including English, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, and more.

■ Privacy Focused
All PDF processing is done locally on your device. Your files are never uploaded to any external servers.
