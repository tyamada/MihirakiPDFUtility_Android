Mihiraki PDF Utility

Intuitive PDF spreading, splitting, and merging for Android.

About the App

Mihiraki PDF Utility is a feature-rich tool designed for readers and creators. Whether you're organizing scanned books (Jisui), merging documents, or reordering pages, our intuitive interface makes it effortless.

Key Features

Spread (Mihiraki) View

View two pages side-by-side. Supports both LTR and RTL reading directions for manga and books.

Smart Splitting

Split double-page spreads into individual pages. Supports vertical and horizontal splitting.

Drag & Drop Reordering

Organize your PDF by simply dragging thumbnails. Move single or multiple pages at once.

Merge & Append

Combine multiple PDF files into a single document easily.

Cross-Device Usage

The app is designed to provide a seamless experience across various Android form factors, including phones, tablets, and desktop environments like ChromeOS.
